# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project is maintained and administrated by a single maintainer. The goal of this project is to have a friendly and productive environment for collaboration. This document is to outline expectations of users and developers.
As the project grows and matures, so will this document. Please check back here every so often to ensure your continued 


## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and other forms of discussion.
- Focus feedback primarily on the work provided and not strictly on the people providing the work.
- Always assume actions are enacted in good faith. If a pull request is rejected, or an issue is closed, it is a decision about the project and not a judgment on you.
- Always ask and or discuss in a public form (GitHub greatly preferred, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md#document-first)) before making a large change. This includes but is not limited to rewrites, restructuring, big features.


## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Spam, and otherwise off-topic discussions.
- Demanding fixes, features, or pressuring maintainers on a timetable or timeline.
- Arguing about issues or discussions after it has been closed. If a decision was made, let it be. However, should a new fruitful topic arise at a later time, respectful discussion is perfectly acceptable in a closed issue and it may be reopened. 
- Posting private information about yourself or others. For the purposes of moderation, even a data disclosure from a consenting party is not allowed. Keep your information private.


## Enforcement

This project has a single maintainer, who is the **sole authority** on all decisions, including conduct and enforcement. There is not a "board of authority" nor otherwise elected members.

- If your behavior violates this Code of Conduct, the maintainer will make an attempt to warn you in private. If a private communication can not be reasonably established, you will be warned publicly.
- Repeated or serious violations will likely result in comments being removed, your account being blocked, or your contributions being revered at the maintainers own discretion.


## Reporting

To report a violation, please contact the maintainer privately via publicmail@armoreddragon.com.


## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces. This includes issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official chat or social channels.
